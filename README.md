# Math-Concepts-for-Developers-2026

The primary goal of the course is to build a foundation of mathematical knowledge. It covers topics such as working with matrices and vectors, the use and purpose of complex numbers, and the relationship between a function's derivative and velocity, all viewed through the lens of new concepts and real-life examples.

## High-School Maths
The goal in this lesson is to explore fundamental mathematical concepts, learn how to set up our environment and connect math with Python. Main topics covered are: math and scientific notation, linear equations and systems of linear equations.

## Linear Algebra
The goal in this lesson is to cover linear algebra implemented with Python. Main topics covered are: polynomials, functions, vectors, matrices and linear transformations.

## Calculus
The goal in this lesson is to connect calculus and mathematical modeling with Python programming. Main topics covered are: limits, derivatives, integrals, calculus in many dimensions and gradient descent.

## Probability and Combinatorics
The goal in this lesson is to connect probability and combinatoric concepts as well as signal processing with Python. Main topics covered are: probability, combinatorics, algebra of events, conditional probability, statistical and common distributions and Central Limit theorem.

## Statistics
The goal in this lesson is to connect statistical concepts with Python. Main topics covered are: descriptive and inferential statistics, sampling methods, properties of statistical distributions, visualizing data, covariance and correlation and common misconceptions.
